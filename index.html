Questions

What do you need from us?

Your trade blotter, custodian statement, capital activity and board-approved pricing file, in whatever format your systems export.

Are you SOC 1 audited?

Not yet. It is on our roadmap. Shadow mode lets you check our work before relying on it.

Do you value our positions?

No. Your board sets the pricing policy and your general partner owns valuation. We apply the approved marks and document them.

How is our data handled?

An NDA and a data processing agreement come first. Files are used only to administer your fund and are deleted on request.

Can we see a sample?

Yes. Write to hello@attest.fund and we'll send a specimen NAV pack and investor statement.
