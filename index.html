How it works

You send your files

Your trade blotter, custodian statement, capital activity and board-approved prices, in whatever format your systems already export.

We reconcile and value

Every trade is matched to your custodian, positions are priced by your board's policy, and fees are worked out for each investor.

You get the documents

A NAV pack for the fund and a capital statement for every investor, with each figure traced back to your files.

How we handle your fund

We never set your marks

Your board sets the pricing policy and your general partner owns valuation. We apply the approved marks and record every step.

Every file fingerprinted

Each input file gets a SHA-256 fingerprint when it arrives, so any NAV we issue can be re-run and checked against the exact files behind it.

Changes come with a reason

If a number moves, we show what changed, which file caused it, and any cash already paid at the old figure.

Your files stay yours

An NDA and a data processing agreement come before anything is sent. Files are used only for your fund and deleted on request.
