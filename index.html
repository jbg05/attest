Approach

How an engagement begins

No migration project and no change to how your fund runs. You send the files you already produce, and we show you what we would have issued.

i.

Send one period

Your trade blotter, custodian statement, capital activity and board-approved pricing file, in whatever format they come out of your systems.

ii.

Compare

We return a NAV pack and a statement for every investor. You set them beside your own numbers, and where they differ, the exception report says why.

iii.

Move when it matches

After a few periods in agreement, Attest can become your administrator of record. If we haven't earned that, you've lost nothing.

Deliverables

What you receive each period

The documents your investors, your auditor and your own operations team ask for, prepared the same way every time.

  1. i.

    NAV pack

    Reconciliation to the custodian with every break named, positions and marks with their source, the NAV waterfall, the allocation across investors and a fingerprint of every input file.

  2. ii.

    Capital statements

    One per investor of record. Opening capital, share of P&L, incentive fee against that investor's own high-water mark, closing capital, and for a partial redemption the amount paid, the holdback and what stays in the fund.

  3. iii.

    Year-to-date statement

    Every contribution, each period's P&L and fees, withdrawals, closing capital and the time-weighted net return, issued to investors who redeemed during the year as well.

  4. iv.

    Exception report

    Every row we couldn't book as supplied, with its line number and what we did about it. A duplicate, a trade the custodian never confirmed, a settlement with no trade behind it.

  5. v.

    Run record

    Every figure at every valuation date, stored with the files that produced it, so any NAV we've issued can be re-performed exactly, for you or for your auditor.

  6. vi.

    Restatement memorandum

    If a number changes, a short memo leads with the determination and then shows the arithmetic, including any redemption already paid at the old NAV, priced again to the cent.

Controls

How we handle your fund

A few commitments that shape how the work is done, and that hold whether or not anyone is checking.

We administer. We don't value.

Your board sets the pricing policy and your general partner owns valuation. We apply the approved marks, reconcile them and record every step.

Changes come with an explanation

When a restated figure appears, the memorandum says what moved, which file caused it and which later periods it flowed into.

Your data, or our error

If a change comes from your files, we say so. If our own software fails to reproduce a number, we say that instead, and it's never passed to you as something to research.

Your files stay yours

Used only to administer your fund, under an NDA and a data processing agreement signed before anything is sent, and deleted on request.
