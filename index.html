What you get

The documents your LPs and auditors ask for.

NAV pack

Reconciliation with every break called out, positions and marks with their sources, the NAV waterfall, and a fingerprint of every input file.

Capital statements

One per investor: opening capital, share of P&L, incentive fee against their own high-water mark, closing capital, redemptions and holdbacks.

Newer instruments

Event contracts on Kalshi and Polymarket, tokenized treasuries and on-chain positions. Most legacy administrators can't reconcile these yet.

Re-performable

Any NAV we issue can be re-run from the exact files behind it. If a number ever changes, you'll know what changed and why.
