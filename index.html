Questions

What do you need from us?

Your trade blotter, custodian statement, capital activity and board-approved pricing file, in any format.

How does shadow mode work?

We run your period alongside your current process. You compare our NAV pack and statements with your own before relying on them.

Are you SOC 1 audited?

Not yet. It is on our roadmap. Shadow mode lets you check our work first.

Do you value our positions?

No. Your board sets the pricing policy. We apply the approved marks and document them.

Which funds is Attest for?

Emerging hedge, quant and digital asset funds, typically under $500M.
